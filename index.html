For planning only. The pilot in command makes the go/no-go call. Follow the AMA Safety Code and the field rules posted at UVA Field: no flying before 8 AM, flying ends at sunset unless your aircraft is lit for night flying, and stay at or below 700 ft AGL. UVA Field is an FAA-Recognized Identification Area (FRIA): aircraft without Remote ID may fly here, but only inside the site boundary and within visual line of sight.
