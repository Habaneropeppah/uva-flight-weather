# FIELD NOTICE for the UVA Flight Weather app
#
# Whatever you type below (on lines that don't start with #) shows as a banner
# at the top of the app. Leave it empty for no banner.
#
# Optional settings, on their own lines ABOVE the message:
#   type: closed       red "Field closed" banner, and the app's verdict shows CLOSED
#                      instead of GO/NO-GO. Without this line it's a blue "Field notice".
#   until: 2026-11-15  the banner disappears automatically after this date
#
# Example:
#   type: closed
#   until: 2026-11-15
#   Field closed Saturday for the fun fly setup. Back open Sunday at 8 AM.
